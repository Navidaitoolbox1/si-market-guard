# SI Market Guard, Store Copy

## English
Name: SI Market Guard
Subtitle: Portfolio risk alerts

SI Market Guard helps investors monitor stocks and ETFs, distinguish ordinary volatility from material risk, define staged buy zones, and receive clear risk-management guidance. It focuses on disciplined monitoring, not market prediction.

For informational purposes only. Not investment advice.

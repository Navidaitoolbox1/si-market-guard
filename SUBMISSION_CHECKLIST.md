# SI Market Guard submission checklist

1. Confirm the individual or business Developer identity that should own the public listing is verified.
2. In the OpenAI Plugins dashboard, choose **Upload new or existing plugin** and upload si-market-guard-v1.0.0.zip.
3. Confirm the uploaded package is version 1.0.0, the listing copy is correct, and the English, French, German, and Persian text is present.
4. Resolve automated metadata findings and wait for the required skill scan to finish successfully.
5. Review the plugin's icon, description, capabilities, onboarding skill, and language behavior.
6. Complete the dashboard's policy attestations and submit the draft for review.
7. After approval, choose **Publish plugin**.

Notes:

- The public publisher name is taken from the verified Developer identity selected in the dashboard.
- A skills-only package does not require an MCP server, reviewer credentials, or an MCP demo recording.
- Do not claim OpenAI endorsement. The plugin does not store a persistent portfolio or execute trades.
- If adding an MCP server later, prepare its separate review requirements and update the privacy, support, website, and terms information before submission.
